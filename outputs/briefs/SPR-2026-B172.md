## Abstract

If the activity of nitrate reductase (NR) in the root epidermis is locally inhibited by salicylic acid (SA) within 15-30 minutes, then the concentration of free auxin (IAA) in the same epidermal cells will increase by 40-80% relative to untreated controls, because SA-induced NR inhibition creates a local nitrate deficit that triggers compensatory auxin biosynthesis to stimulate lateral root initiation as an alternative nitrogen foraging strategy.

The proposed mechanism involves 4 causal steps: (1) SA (50 µM) binds to and inhibits nitrate reductase (NR) activity specifically in -> (2) Local NR inhibition reduces nitrate assimilation, decreasing glutamine synthesis -> (3) The N-deficit signal upregulates the expression and activity of YUCCA auxin bios -> (4) Elevated epidermal IAA is transported to pericycle cells, initiating lateral roo.

Literature grounding on 0 verified references yields a novelty score of 0.5 (novel). A 3-phase experimental protocol (budget: €25k-120k, timeline: 8-14 months) is proposed, starting with in silico validation. A panel of 5 expert reviewers reached a consensus score of 5.6/10.

## 1. Hypothese et mecanisme propose

### 1.1 Formulation formelle

If the activity of nitrate reductase (NR) in the root epidermis is locally inhibited by salicylic acid (SA) within 15-30 minutes, then the concentration of free auxin (IAA) in the same epidermal cells will increase by 40-80% relative to untreated controls, because SA-induced NR inhibition creates a local nitrate deficit that triggers compensatory auxin biosynthesis to stimulate lateral root initiation as an alternative nitrogen foraging strategy.

### 1.3 Chaine causale

1. Step 1: SA (50 µM) binds to and inhibits nitrate reductase (NR) activity specifically in root epidermal cells within 15 minutes, measured by a 30-50% reduction in fluorescence intensity from a NR-targeting ABP (e.g., based on a triazine scaffold with a fluorophosphonate warhead).
1. Step 2: Local NR inhibition reduces nitrate assimilation, decreasing glutamine synthesis by 20-40% in epidermal cells (measured via GS-targeting ABP), creating a transient local N-deficit signal.
1. Step 3: The N-deficit signal upregulates the expression and activity of YUCCA auxin biosynthesis enzymes in the epidermis within 30 minutes, increasing local free IAA concentration by 40-80%.
1. Step 4: Elevated epidermal IAA is transported to pericycle cells, initiating lateral root primordia formation within 48 hours, increasing density by 25-60% compared to controls.

**Hypotheses cles :**

- The designed ABPs (for NR, GS) are specific, membrane-permeable, and do not alter endogenous enzyme activity or cell viability by more than 10% at working concentrations (≤10 µM).
- The bioorthogonal click chemistry (e.g., SPAAC) labeling efficiency is ≥70% in living root tissues and correlates linearly (R² > 0.85) with in vitro enzymatic activity.
- The primary site of SA-NR interaction and subsequent signaling is the root epidermis, not the cortex or stele.
- The auxin biosensor DII-VENUS provides a quantitative (nM range) and spatially resolved readout of free IAA changes in the epidermis with a temporal resolution of <5 minutes.

**Inconnues identifiees :**

- We do not know the molecular identity of the sensor linking local N-assimilation status to YUCCA gene expression.
- We do not know the threshold level of NR inhibition (% activity loss) required to trigger the auxin response.
- We do not know if the mechanism is conserved in other plant species (e.g., cereals) or is specific to Arabidopsis.

### 1.4 Conditions aux limites

- **SA concentration must be ≤100 µM and exposure time ≤2 hours.** — Higher/longer SA treatments induce systemic acquired resistance and apoptosis, confounding the specific NR-inhibition phenotype.
- **Experiments must be conducted on 5-7 day-old Arabidopsis seedlings grown vertically on agar plates.** — This developmental stage exhibits maximal root growth responsiveness to nitrate and hormone signals; older plants have established root architecture with different sensitivities.
- **ABP concentration must be ≤10 µM and incubation time ≤60 min.** — Pilot toxicity assays (Phase 0) must confirm that these parameters maintain cell viability >90% (FDA staining) and do not alter root growth rate by >10% over 24h.
- **The mechanism is testable only in root zones 1-3 mm from the tip (elongation zone).** — This zone contains the most metabolically active epidermal cells and is the primary site for nitrate sensing and lateral root initiation.

### 1.5 Cadre theorique

Nutrient-Foraging Theory (Bloom et al., 1985) integrated with Hormonal Crosstalk Signaling Networks, specifically the Nitrogen-Auxin Feedback Loop hypothesis.

## 4. Protocole experimental

**Timeline globale**: 8-14 months
**Budget global**: €25k-120k

### 4.1 Phase 1 — In Silico Validation & Molecular Docking

**Objectif**: To computationally assess the feasibility of SA directly binding and inhibiting Arabidopsis Nitrate Reductase (NR), and to model the downstream metabolic perturbation leading to a predicted auxin increase.

**Methodologie**: 1. **Molecular Docking & Dynamics**: Use AlphaFold3 to model the full-length structure of Arabidopsis NR (NIA1/NIA2). Perform molecular docking of salicylic acid (SA) against the Mo-cofactor (Molybdenum Cofactor) binding site, FAD domain, and potential allosteric sites using AutoDock Vina or Glide (Schrödinger). Run short (50-100 ns) MD simulations (GROMACS/NAMD) of NR with/without SA to assess binding stability and conformational changes affecting substrate (nitrate) access.
2. **Constraint-Based Metabolic Modeling**: Build a compartmentalized (cytosol, epidermis) metabolic network for Arabidopsis root epidermis using the AraCore model as a template. Simulate the effect of a 30-50% reduction in NR flux (step 1) on central nitrogen metabolism and ATP/redox balance using Flux Balance Analysis (FBA) in COBRApy. Specifically, test if the predicted local glutamine deficit (step 2) emerges as a necessary consequence.
3. **Gene Regulatory Network Inference**: Use existing Arabidopsis root single-cell RNA-seq datasets (e.g., from SRA: SRP158991) to infer a gene co-expression network centered on NIA1, NIA2, and YUCCA genes. Analyze this network (Cytoscape) for shared transcription factors or cis-elements (using PlantRegMap/AGRIS) that could act as the unknown N-status sensor (Known Unknown #1).

- Cout: €0-1,500 (primarily cloud/HPC costs if no local cluster)
- Duree: 4-6 weeks
- Equipement: High-performance computing cluster (≥32 cores, 128GB RAM)
- Logiciels: AlphaFold3 (via ColabFold), Schrödinger Suite (academic license) or AutoDock Vina, GROMACS 2023, COBRApy, Cytoscape, R/Bioconductor

**Criteres de succes :**

- SA-NR Binding Affinity: Predicted ΔG ≤ -6.0 kcal/mol (suggestive of specific binding) AND stable binding pose in MD simulation (RMSD < 2.5 Å over 50 ns).
- Metabolic Perturbation: FBA simulation predicts a ≥15% decrease in glutamine synthesis flux upon 40% NR flux reduction.
- Network Connectivity: Identification of ≥3 transcription factors with significant co-expression (Pearson's r > 0.7) with both NIA and YUCCA gene families.

- **GO**: At least 2/3 success criteria are met, providing a plausible computational basis for the hypothesized mechanism.
- **NO-GO**: 0/3 criteria are met (e.g., SA shows no stable binding to NR, FBA predicts no glutamine deficit, no transcriptional link found).
- **PIVOT**: Only 1 criterion is met. Example: SA binds NR but no metabolic link is found. Pivot to test if SA inhibits NR indirectly (e.g., via post-translational modification) in Phase 2.

### 4.2 Phase 2 — Minimal Experimental: ABP Validation & Core Response

**Objectif**: To physically test the core causal chain (Steps 1 & 3) in living seedlings: Does SA rapidly inhibit epidermal NR activity, and does this precede a measurable increase in epidermal auxin?

**Methodologie**: 1. **ABP Synthesis & Validation**: Synthesize or source a triazine-fluorophosphonate ABP (e.g., analogous to JACS 2012, 134, 10329) targeting NR. Validate in vitro using purified Arabidopsis NR (Agrisera) to establish a linear correlation (R² > 0.85) between fluorescence (after click-chemistry with Azide-Cy5) and enzyme activity.
2. **Live-Root Imaging Protocol**: Grow 5-day-old Arabidopsis Col-0 seedlings (expressing nuclear-localized DII-VENUS auxin sensor) vertically on ½ MS + 10 mM KNO3. Mount seedling in microfluidic chamber (e.g., CellASIC ONIX) for perfusion. Perfuse with 10 µM NR-ABP for 30 min, wash, then perfuse with 50 µM SA (or mock) for 30 min. Perform click-chemistry labeling in situ (SPAAC with DBCO-Cy5).
3. **Quantitative Imaging**: Use confocal microscopy (Zeiss LSM 880) to simultaneously quantify: a) NR-ABP-Cy5 fluorescence intensity in the epidermis of the elongation zone (1-3 mm from tip), and b) DII-VENUS nuclear fluorescence ratio (indicative of free IAA). Image at T=0 (pre-SA), 15, 30, 60 min post-SA perfusion. Analyze fluorescence intensity per µm² of epidermal cell area (FIJI/ImageJ).

- Cout: €8k-14k (ABP synthesis: €3-6k, consumables, microscope time)
- Duree: 2-3 months
- Equipement: Confocal microscope with 488nm/561nm/633nm lasers, Microfluidic perfusion system (CellASIC ONIX), HPLC/MS for ABP purity check
- Logiciels: FIJI/ImageJ, Zen Blue, GraphPad Prism

**Criteres de succes :**

- NR Inhibition by SA: ≥30% decrease in NR-ABP fluorescence intensity in epidermis at 15-30 min post-SA treatment vs. mock (p < 0.01, n≥15 epidermal cells from 5 roots).
- Auxin Increase: ≥40% decrease in DII-VENUS nuclear fluorescence ratio (indicating IAA increase) in the same epidermal cells by 30-60 min post-SA.
- Temporal Order: NR inhibition (significant drop) must be detectable BEFORE a significant auxin increase (sequential paired t-test, p<0.05).

- **GO**: All 3 success criteria are met, confirming the core spatiotemporal sequence of the hypothesis.
- **NO-GO**: Criterion 1 (NR inhibition) is not met, falsifying the initial trigger of the proposed mechanism.
- **PIVOT**: Criterion 1 is met but Criterion 2 (auxin increase) is not. Pivot Phase 3 to investigate alternative downstream outputs of local NR inhibition (e.g., reactive oxygen species, other hormones).

### 4.3 Phase 3 — Full Experimental: Mechanistic Dissection & Phenotypic Validation

**Objectif**: To rigorously validate the entire proposed causal chain, establish necessity and sufficiency, and link the molecular events to the phenotypic output (lateral root initiation).

**Methodologie**: 1. **Genetic & Pharmacological Dissection**: Repeat Phase 2 live-imaging protocol with: a) nia1 nia2 double mutant (Prediction 1), b) WT co-treated with auxin transport inhibitor NPA (1 µM) (Prediction 2), c) WT under high (50 mM) vs. low (0.1 mM) nitrate (Prediction 3). Include glutamine synthetase (GS)-ABP to measure Step 2.
2. **Spatially Resolved Metabolomics**: Use laser-capture microdissection (LCM) to collect epidermal cells from the elongation zone of seedlings treated as in Phase 2 (SA vs. mock, 30 min). Perform targeted LC-MS/MS to quantify absolute levels of IAA, glutamine, glutamate, and nitrate.
3. **Phenotypic Endpoint Quantification**: Grow seedlings for 48h post-SA treatment (50 µM, 2h pulse) under different nitrate regimes. Clear and stain roots with propidium iodide. Use high-throughput automated microscopy (e.g., Phenopsis) and Root System Analyzer software to quantify lateral root primordia density in the primary root zone where treatment occurred.
4. **Sufficiency Test**: Use a chemical-genetic approach (e.g., cell-specific expression of a dexamethasone-inducible NR silencing construct) to inhibit epidermal NR without SA, and measure IAA and lateral root initiation.

- Cout: €60k-100k+ (major costs: LC-MS/MS time, LCM consumables, labor)
- Duree: 8-12 months
- Equipement: LCM system (e.g., Leica LMD7), LC-MS/MS system (Triple Quadrupole), High-throughput phenotyping scopes, Climate-controlled growth chambers
- Logiciels: Skyline (for MS data), Root System Analyzer, Statistical software (R, Prism)

**Criteres de succes :**

- Genetic Necessity (Prediction 1): nia1 nia2 mutant shows no significant SA-induced IAA increase (change within ±15% of baseline), while NR-ABP signal is absent (confirming target).
- Auxin Transport Requirement (Prediction 2): NPA co-treatment reduces SA-induced lateral root primordia density to within 10% of control levels, while NR inhibition by SA is still observed.
- Nitrate Dependence (Prediction 3): Under high nitrate (50 mM), SA-induced IAA increase is attenuated to ≤25%, significantly lower than the ≥40% increase under 10 mM nitrate.

- **GO**: All 3 success criteria are met, providing strong, multi-faceted validation of the hypothesis. Proceed to manuscript preparation and proposal for follow-up studies (e.g., identifying the N-sensor).
- **NO-GO**: Two or more of the key falsifiable predictions (1, 2, 3) are disproven, indicating the core hypothesis is incorrect.
- **PIVOT**: Only one prediction is disproven. Example: Mechanism works in WT but not in nr mutant (Prediction 1 holds), but is not nitrate-dependent (Prediction 3 fails). Pivot to explore SA-NR-auxin pathway as a nitrate-independent stress response.

### 6.1 Consensus

- Le protocole expérimental est bien structuré, ambitieux et méthodologiquement rigoureux, avec une approche séquentielle (GO/NO-GO/PIVOT) jugée exemplaire pour gérer le risque et les ressources.
- L'hypothèse centrale est considérée comme hautement spéculative et mécanistiquement sous-étayée, contredisant en partie la littérature établie sur la biologie du SA et la signalisation de l'azote. Le lien causal entre l'inhibition de la NR épidermique et la biosynthèse locale d'auxine manque de plausibilité et de composants de signalisation identifiés.
- La technologie des sondes d'activité bioorthogonales (ABP) est reconnue comme innovante mais constitue un point de risque technique majeur. Sa spécificité, son efficacité et sa non-perturbation in vivo doivent être rigoureusement validées avant de fonder toute la chaîne causale sur cette méthode.

### 6.2 Points de desaccord

- Le méthodologiste (score 8.2) estime que les faiblesses sont surmontables par des modifications critiques mais mineures (analyse de puissance, contrôles, aveugle), tandis que le domain_expert, le contrarian et l'industrialist (scores 4.5) jugent les problèmes fondamentaux (mécanisme, plausibilité, traduction) trop graves pour une acceptation sans révision majeure.
- L'évaluation du potentiel de translation diffère radicalement : l'industrialist voit un chemin long et incertain vers un produit, tandis que le funding_strategist identifie des opportunités de financement pour la recherche fondamentale, considérant le projet comme aligné avec les tendances scientifiques malgré son TRL bas.

### 6.3 Critical path

Le facteur le plus déterminant pour le succès/échec de cette hypothèse est la validation préalable et indépendante du lien causal central SA → inhibition de la NR → déficit local en N → biosynthèse d'auxine. Cela doit être démontré en découplant la validation mécanistique du développement risqué des ABPs. L'utilisation d'outils génétiques (mutants, knockdowns spécifiques) et d'inhibiteurs chimiques établis pour créer un déficit en N localisé, indépendamment du SA, est essentielle pour tester la plausibilité du cœur de l'hypothèse avant tout investissement dans les technologies de cartographie avancées.

**Recommandation finale**: Le panel reconnaît la qualité de la conception expérimentale mais exprime des préoccupations majeures et unanimes sur la plausibilité mécanistique de l'hypothèse et les risques techniques associés aux ABPs. Un rejet pur est évité grâce à la structure robuste du protocole et au potentiel de la technologie. Cependant, un consensus se dégage pour une révision substantielle : l'hypothèse doit être retravaillée pour intégrer les connaissances établies sur la signalisation de l'azote et du SA, et le plan doit être réorganisé pour valider le lien causal fondamental avec des outils conventionnels avant de déployer les méthodes de pointe à haut risque. Une itération de révision est accordée.
