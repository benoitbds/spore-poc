## Abstract

If controlled mechanical vibration is applied to a continuous flow reactor handling a solid-liquid suspension, then the critical volumetric solids fraction (φ_c) at which jamming occurs at a constriction will increase by a statistically significant and reproducible margin, because vibration reduces the effective interparticle friction and contact lifetime, thereby disrupting the formation of stable force chains and arches responsible for clogging.

## 1. Hypothese et mecanisme propose

### 1.1 Formulation formelle

If controlled mechanical vibration is applied to a continuous flow reactor handling a solid-liquid suspension, then the critical volumetric solids fraction (φ_c) at which jamming occurs at a constriction will increase by a statistically significant and reproducible margin, because vibration reduces the effective interparticle friction and contact lifetime, thereby disrupting the formation of stable force chains and arches responsible for clogging.

### 1.2 Variables

**Variables independantes :**

| Variable | Type | Plage | Unite |
|----------|------|-------|-------|
| Dimensionless vibration intensity (Γ) | continuous | 0.1-10 | dimensionless (Γ = aω²/g, where a is amplitude, ω is angular frequency, g is gravity) |
| Fluid dynamic viscosity (η) | continuous | 0.001-1.0 | Pa·s |
| Orifice-to-particle diameter ratio (D/d_p) | continuous | 2-10 | dimensionless |

**Variables dependantes :**

| Variable | Type | Direction attendue | Unite |
|----------|------|-------------------|-------|
| Critical volumetric solids fraction for jamming (φ_c) | continuous | increase | dimensionless (vol solid/vol total) |

### 1.3 Chaine causale

1. Step 1: Applied vibration (Γ) transmits kinetic energy to solid particles, increasing their transient kinetic temperature and reducing the effective contact lifetime between neighboring particles.
1. Step 2: Reduced contact lifetime and increased particle agitation lower the effective interparticle friction (μ_eff), measurable via shear cell tests or inferred from a decrease in the dynamic angle of repose.
1. Step 3: The reduction in μ_eff destabilizes the formation of persistent force chains and arches at the reactor constriction (orifice), thereby increasing the critical solids fraction (φ_c) at which a stable jam can form and extending the mean time between clogging events (τ_clog).

**Hypotheses cles :**

- The primary clogging mechanism is arching/jamming due to frictional and geometric constraints, not adhesive binding or chemical deposition.
- Vibration energy is effectively coupled into the solid phase; fluid viscosity (η) primarily governs energy dissipation and damping, not complete decoupling.
- Particle properties (size, shape, hardness) and surface chemistry remain constant over experimental timescales; no significant wear, attrition, or surface adsorption alters interparticle forces.

**Inconnues identifiees :**

- The quantitative relationship between Γ, fluid η, and the energy damping coefficient for the solid phase in a confined, flowing suspension.
- Whether there exists an optimal Γ that maximizes φ_c before inducing particle segregation, fluid cavitation, or reactor fatigue.
- The long-term evolution of particle surface properties (e.g., due to abrasion or adsorption) under continuous vibration and flow, and its impact on μ_eff.

### 1.4 Conditions aux limites

- **Particle cohesion (characterized by granular Bond number, Bo_g) must be low to moderate: Bo_g < 1, where Bo_g = F_cohesive / F_weight.** — The proposed mechanism targets frictional/geometric jamming. Highly cohesive powders (Bo_g >> 1) clog primarily via strong adhesive bonds, which may not be disrupted by the vibration intensities considered and require different mitigation strategies (e.g., aeration).
- **Orifice-to-particle diameter ratio D/d_p > 2.** — For D/d_p ≤ 2, single-particle clogging dominates, which is insensitive to vibration-induced bulk fluidization and requires different design rules.
- **Vibration frequency (f) is below the system's resonant frequency and above the quasi-static regime: 10 Hz < f < 1000 Hz.** — Frequencies too low act as quasi-static loading; frequencies too high may cause fluid cavitation, particle decoupling, or structural resonance in the reactor hardware.

### 1.5 Cadre theorique

Granular Mechanics and Jamming Phase Transition, extended to damped, liquid-suspended granular systems under forced vibration.

## 4. Protocole experimental

**Timeline globale**: 8-14 months
**Budget global**: €25k-120k

### 4.1 Phase 1 — In Silico Validation

**Objectif**: To rapidly test the core causal chain (Γ → μ_eff reduction → φ_c increase) using discrete element method (DEM) simulations coupled with computational fluid dynamics (CFD) for a damped granular suspension, and to identify promising parameter ranges (Γ, η, D/d_p) before physical experiments.

**Methodologie**: 1. **Model Setup**: Build a 3D DEM-CFD model in LIGGGHTS-PUBLIC coupled with OpenFOAM (via CFDEMcoupling). Use a coarse-grained DEM approach to handle ~50,000 spherical particles (d_p=1mm) to balance accuracy and computational cost. Implement a Hertz-Mindlin contact model with a rolling friction modifier. The fluid phase is modeled via the volume-averaged Navier-Stokes equations. 2. **Virtual Experiment**: Simulate a hopper-like geometry with a bottom orifice (D/d_p variable). Implement a pressure-driven flow of the suspension. Apply a sinusoidal vibration to the entire domain walls (Γ = aω²/g). 3. **Parameter Sweep**: Run simulations for Γ = [0, 0.5, 1, 2, 5] at fixed η=0.01 Pa·s and D/d_p=5. For each condition, incrementally increase the solids fraction (φ) until a stable arch forms and flow stops. Record φ_c. 4. **Mechanism Probe**: From the same simulations, extract particle contact lifetimes and force chain network statistics (using network analysis tools in OVITO). Calculate a proxy for μ_eff from the shear stress to normal stress ratio in a defined shear zone near the orifice.

- Cout: €0-2k (primarily cloud/HPC credits if in-house cluster unavailable)
- Duree: 4-6 weeks
- Equipement: High-performance computing cluster (≥ 32 cores, 128GB RAM)
- Logiciels: LIGGGHTS-PUBLIC, OpenFOAM v10, CFDEMcoupling, OVITO, Python (for analysis & automation)

**Criteres de succes :**

- Statistical significance of φ_c increase: Simulated φ_c at Γ≥1 is > φ_c at Γ=0 with p < 0.05 (one-way ANOVA on 10 replicate runs per condition).
- Monotonic trend in μ_eff proxy: Spearman's rank correlation coefficient (ρ) between Γ and the μ_eff proxy is negative and significant (p < 0.05).

- **GO**: Both success criteria are met, confirming the simulated trend of increasing φ_c and decreasing μ_eff with Γ. The predicted φ_c increase is ≥ 10 percentage points.
- **NO-GO**: No significant increase in φ_c is observed, or μ_eff increases with Γ. This would falsify the core mechanism under simulated ideal conditions, suggesting a fundamental flaw.
- **PIVOT**: φ_c increases but μ_eff does not decrease, suggesting an alternative mechanism (e.g., vibration-induced dilation). Proceed to Phase 2 but expand measurements to include local volume fraction.

### 4.2 Phase 2 — Minimal Experimental Validation

**Objectif**: To physically confirm the key prediction (Prediction 1) using a benchtop, optically accessible flow cell with controlled vibration, establishing a proof-of-concept for vibration-induced clogging suppression.

**Methodologie**: 1. **Apparatus**: Construct a transparent (PMMA/glass) vertical flow cell (e.g., a modified silo) with a replaceable orifice plate at the bottom. Mount the cell on an electromagnetic shaker (e.g., TiraVib TV 51110) controlled by a function generator and amplifier. Use a syringe pump or pressurized reservoir to drive flow. 2. **Materials**: Use model system: monodisperse glass beads (d_p = 500 μm, Bo_g < 0.1) suspended in a density-matched, Newtonian fluid (e.g., water-glycerol mixture, η ≈ 0.01 Pa·s). Refractive index matching (RIM) may be used if necessary for imaging. 3. **Measurement**: For each condition (Γ = [0, 1, 2, 3, 5]), conduct a 'jamming assay'. Gradually increase the solids fraction φ in the feed reservoir. For each φ, initiate flow and record (via camera) until a permanent clog forms or a 5-minute timeout. Define clogging as complete flow cessation for >30s. The φ_c is the lowest φ at which clogging occurs in ≥ 8 out of 10 trials. Conduct ≥100 trials per condition. 4. **Data Acquisition**: Record high-speed video (500 fps) of the orifice region. Log pressure transducer data upstream of the orifice and accelerometer data on the cell.

- Cout: €8k-15k (primarily for shaker, camera, and cell fabrication)
- Duree: 2-3 months
- Equipement: Electromagnetic shaker with amplifier, Function generator, Transparent flow cell with orifice plates, High-speed camera (e.g., Photron FASTCAM Mini AX), Syringe pump or pressure-regulated feed system, Pressure transducer, Accelerometer, Digital scale for flow rate
- Logiciels: MATLAB or Python for data acquisition (e.g., via NI-DAQmx), ImageJ/TrackMate for particle tracking (optional), Statistical software (JMP, R)

**Criteres de succes :**

- Increase in φ_c: φ_c at Γ=3 is ≥ 15 percentage points higher than φ_c at Γ=0, with non-overlapping 95% confidence intervals (from logistic regression fit of jamming probability vs. φ).
- Statistical significance: One-way ANOVA on φ_c estimates (from logistic fit) across Γ conditions yields p < 0.05, with Dunnett's test confirming Γ>0 conditions differ from Γ=0.

- **GO**: Both criteria met. The core effect is experimentally confirmed with a substantial and significant increase in φ_c.
- **NO-GO**: No significant increase in φ_c is observed, or the increase is negligible (<5 percentage points). This falsifies the primary hypothesis under controlled lab conditions.
- **PIVOT**: Effect is significant but smaller than predicted (5-15 percentage points). Proceed to Phase 3 but focus on optimizing Γ and investigating damping effects (Prediction 3) more deeply.

### 4.3 Phase 3 — Full Experimental Protocol

**Objectif**: To fully validate the hypothesis and its underlying mechanism across the defined parameter space, generating publishable data on φ_c, τ_clog, and μ_eff, and establishing design guidelines.

**Methodologie**: 1. **Multi-Factorial DOE**: Conduct a full factorial or response surface design exploring Γ (0-10), η (0.001-0.5 Pa·s), and D/d_p (2-10). 2. **Primary Metrics**: For each condition, determine φ_c (as in Phase 2) and measure τ_clog during extended runs at a fixed, high solids fraction (φ = 0.9 * φ_c,static). 3. **Mechanistic Probes**: a) **μ_eff Measurement**: Use a custom vibrating shear cell (modified Schulze ring shear tester mounted on shaker) to measure μ_eff of the saturated granular material under vibration. b) **Flow Field & Arching**: Use Refractive Index Matching (RIM) with Planar Laser-Induced Fluorescence (PLIF) or Particle Tracking Velocimetry (PTV) to visualize internal flow and arch formation dynamics. c) **Energy Transfer**: Use calorimetry (measuring temperature rise of isolated cell) or PIV on tracer particles to quantify kinetic energy input to solid vs. fluid phases. 4. **Long-Term Test**: A 24-hour continuous run at optimal Γ to check for reactor fatigue, particle degradation, or effect decay.

- Cout: €15k-100k+ (depending on existing lab infrastructure; major costs: shear tester, PIV laser/camera, RIM fluids)
- Duree: 6-12 months
- Equipement: Phase 2 apparatus (enhanced), Ring shear tester with vibration mount, Laser sheet generator & high-sensitivity camera for PIV/PLIF, RIM-compatible flow cell and fluid ingredients, Calorimetry setup (thermocouples, data logger), Environmental chamber (for temperature control of viscous fluids)
- Logiciels: LaVision DaVis for PIV/PTV, Design of Experiments software (JMP, Minitab), Advanced statistical and visualization packages (R, Python)

**Criteres de succes :**

- Validation of all three falsifiable predictions: 1) φ_c increases with Γ (p<0.05, effect size ≥15pp). 2) μ_eff decreases with Γ (p<0.05, slope negative). 3) (τ_clog,vib/τ_clog,static) decreases with η (significant interaction term, p<0.05).
- Mechanistic consistency: The reduction in μ_eff (Criterion 2) statistically mediates the relationship between Γ and the increase in φ_c (Criterion 1), as shown by a significant mediation analysis (Sobel test or bootstrapping).

- **GO**: All success criteria are met. The hypothesis is fully validated. Next steps: pilot testing in a real reactor prototype and manuscript preparation.
- **NO-GO**: Prediction 1 or 2 is conclusively falsified, or the mediation analysis shows no link between μ_eff and φ_c. The core mechanism is invalid.
- **PIVOT**: Predictions 1 and 2 hold, but Prediction 3 does not (vibration effective even at high η). This is a positive finding; pivot to investigate the unexpected damping resilience and refine the theory.

### 6.1 Consensus

- L'hypothèse est théoriquement fondée, mécaniquement plausible et s'attaque à un problème industriel concret et coûteux (clogging).
- Le protocole expérimental proposé est jugé rigoureux, séquentiel et basé sur des prédictions falsifiables, ce qui constitue une force majeure.
- Le projet est au stade exploratoire (TRL 1-2) et nécessite une validation expérimentale préalable avant toute considération de déploiement ou de financement à grande échelle.

### 6.2 Points de desaccord

- L'efficacité du transfert d'énergie vibratoire dans une suspension liquide (damping) est le point de fracture principal. Le Contrarian et le Domain Expert jugent cet obstacle potentiellement rédhibitoire, tandis que le Methodologist et les autres estiment que le protocole est conçu pour le tester.
- La nature dominante du mécanisme de colmatage (friction vs. adhésion) n'est pas tranchée. Les experts de domaine doutent de l'applicabilité aux suspensions cohésives, un point que le protocole initial ne couvre pas explicitement.
- L'impact de la vibration sur la dynamique granulaire est interprété différemment : comme un outil de fluidisation ciblée (hypothèse de base) ou comme un risque de provoquer des effets pervers (ségrégation, usure) pouvant aggraver le colmatage (Contrarian).

### 6.3 Critical path

La démonstration expérimentale que l'application de vibration (Γ) dans un milieu liquide visqueux réduit effectivement le frottement interparticulaire (μ_eff) dans les conditions d'écoulement, et que cette réduction est le mécanisme causal conduisant à une augmentation mesurable de φ_c. L'échec à établir ce lien causal invaliderait la chaîne d'argumentation centrale.

**Recommandation finale**: Le panel recommande la publication d'un brief de recherche pour cette hypothèse. Elle est suffisamment fondée, testable et pertinente pour justifier une investigation. Cependant, la voie critique identifiée exige que la Phase 1 (in silico) et la Phase 2 (expérimentale) soient exécutées avec une attention particulière portée à la quantification du couplage énergétique en milieu liquide et à l'exclusion des mécanismes adhésifs. Les préoccupations du Contrarian concernant le damping et les dynamiques perverses doivent être intégrées comme des risques explicites à tester et à monitorer dans le protocole. Le financement doit cibler des programmes exploratoires (type ANR PE) pour générer ces preuves de concept décisives.
